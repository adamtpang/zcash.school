zcash.school is built by Adam Pang ([adampang.com](https://adampang.com))
as part of the Zcash Network School (ZNS) team. Every channel below is
one the site already uses; pick the one that fits what you want to say.

## Ways to reach the team

- **The feedback form:** [zcash.school/feedback](/feedback/). The
  feedback text is the only required field; name and email are
  optional. Submissions go to the Zcash Network School team's shared
  Google Sheet.
- **Public, on the forum:**
  [forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-network-school/55269](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-network-school/55269).
  The forum thread is best for substantive discussion or anything you
  would want other attendees and the Zcash Foundation to see.
- **In person:** drop into the next [office hours](/ns/#schedule)
  at Network School.
- **An issue on the repo:**
  [github.com/adamtpang/zcash.school/issues](https://github.com/adamtpang/zcash.school/issues)
  for a concrete bug or content fix on the site. Corrections ship fast.

## Before you write

The forms on zcash.school never ask for seed phrases, private keys,
or funds, so do not include them in a message. zcash.school is an
educational course, not a wallet, an exchange, a price page, or
investment advice.

See also: [About](/about/) and [Privacy](/privacy/).
