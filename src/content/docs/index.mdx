## Syllabus

Completeness. Soundness. Zero-knowledge. Vibes.

This is a free, opinionated, end-to-end course in **Zcash**: the privacy-preserving
cryptocurrency that uses zero-knowledge proofs to keep transaction amounts and
parties private by default. We assume nothing. By the end you'll understand:

- Why financial privacy matters and what Bitcoin gave up
- How zero-knowledge proofs work, including zk-SNARKs and the trustless **Halo 2** system
- How Zcash structures addresses, pools, notes, and nullifiers, and where it's headed with **Project Tachyon**
- How to actually use Zcash, wallets, shielded transactions, selective disclosure, and accepting it in your business
- The orgs, governance process, and economics of the network

## Who this is for

Curious newcomers, Bitcoin holders who looked at their public balance and felt
weird, developers who want a solid mental model before reading the spec,
journalists or policy folks who need accurate primary-source material on how
shielded money works, and anyone who has ever typed the words "encrypted Bitcoin"
in a chat and meant it as a compliment.

## About zcash.school

zcash.school is a free, opinionated course in Zcash covering privacy, zero
knowledge proofs, and how shielded money actually works. The course assumes no
background and links to primary sources wherever they exist, so a curious
person can finish it in an afternoon and come out understanding zero-knowledge
money.

zcash.school is one deliverable of the Zcash Network School (ZNS), a 90-day
program supported by a Zcash Foundation community grant and running at Network
School in Malaysia. The Zcash Foundation funds the ZNS program; the editorial
work and opinions on zcash.school belong to the team that writes it.

zcash.school is the curriculum layer of a small family of sister projects:
[zcash.me](https://zcash.me) helps you get a wallet and get moving, and
[zcashnames.com](https://zcashnames.com) adds human-readable names instead of
raw `u1...` address strings. Full team detail lives on the [Team page](/team/).

zcash.school is not affiliated with the Electric Coin Company, the Zcash
Foundation, or ZODL. The site links to those organizations but does not speak
for them, and it is not a wallet, an exchange, a price page, or investment
advice.

The Zcash Network School team posts monthly to the
[Zcash community forum](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-network-school/55269)
so anyone can see what the grant funds. The site source is public on
[GitHub](https://github.com/adamtpang/zcash.school), and anyone who finds an
error can open an issue there. More on the [About page](/about/).
