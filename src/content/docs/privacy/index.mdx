zcash.school is a static course site with two small forms. This page
lists everything that actually runs on it, so you can decide what to
share. Questions or corrections: see [Contact](/contact/).

## Analytics (PostHog)

zcash.school uses PostHog (US cloud, `us.i.posthog.com`) to count
visits. The PostHog script records page views, when you leave a page,
Core Web Vitals performance timings, heatmap data (where on a page
people click and how far they scroll), and clicks that do nothing
("dead clicks"). General autocapture of every click, session
recording, and surveys are turned off in the site's code. PostHog
stores a random identifier in a first-party cookie and local storage so
repeat visits can be counted; visitors are not identified by name or
email.

PostHog does not receive what you type into the forms on this site.

## The airdrop form (/airdrops and /claim)

The airdrop form collects your public Zcash unified address (`u1...`),
your email, and an optional event name. Your browser's user-agent
string is sent along with it. The form never asks for seed phrases,
private keys, or funds.

Each submission is sent to a Google Apps Script that writes it to the
Zcash Network School team's Google Sheet. A copy of the submission,
including your IP address, is also written to the site's server logs
on Vercel so a failed delivery can be recovered. The email is used for
the shielded newsletter and, rarely, a follow-up if a claim bounces.

## The feedback form (/feedback)

The feedback form collects your feedback, an optional rating, an
optional page or topic, and an optional name and email, plus your
browser's user-agent string. It goes to the same Google Apps Script and
Google Sheet as the airdrop form, and a copy including your IP address
is written to the Vercel server logs.

## Hosting (Vercel)

Vercel hosts zcash.school and runs the two form endpoints. Like any web
host, Vercel processes your IP address and request details to serve
pages. The form endpoints keep your IP address in memory for about a
minute to limit repeated submissions.

## Fonts (Google Fonts)

The Space Grotesk heading font is loaded from Google Fonts
(`fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com`), so your browser
requests it from Google, which receives your IP address and browser
details like any web request.

## What zcash.school does not do

- No advertising trackers, no sold or rented lists, no third-party
  form provider or CRM.
- No account system and no payments on this site.
- No request for seed phrases, private keys, or funds.

To have a form submission removed, ask through any channel on the
[Contact](/contact/) page.
